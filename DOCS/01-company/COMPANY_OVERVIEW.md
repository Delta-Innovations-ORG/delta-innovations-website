## Who we are

**Delta Innovations** is a Pakistan and Egypt-based software development company helping startups and businesses build scalable, secure, and user-focused digital products.

We deliver web development, mobile applications, backend systems and APIs, DevOps and cloud deployment, AI/ML solutions, data analytics, cybersecurity improvements, UI/UX design, and digital transformation consulting.

## One-line pitch

We turn business ideas into scalable digital products through clear requirements, disciplined engineering, secure development, and transparent delivery.

## What makes us different

- **Written requirements before code** — scope documented before development starts
- **Security by default** — env vars, HTTPS, least-privilege, no secrets in repos
- **Transparent GitHub workflow** — version control, review, traceable delivery
- **Practical engineering** — maintainable solutions, not over-engineering
