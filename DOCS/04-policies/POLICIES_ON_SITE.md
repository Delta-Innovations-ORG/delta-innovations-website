# Policies on the Website

Legal and policy content is rendered on the **live website**, not duplicated in full here.

## Canonical source

Policy text lives in [`src/content/policies.ts`](../../src/content/policies.ts) and is displayed via [`PolicyLayout`](../../src/components/ui/PolicyLayout.tsx).

## Routes

| Policy | URL |
|--------|-----|
| Privacy Policy | `/privacy` |
| Terms & Conditions | `/terms` |
| Refund Policy | `/refund` |
| Cookie Policy | `/cookies` |
| Security Policy | `/security` |
| Code of Conduct | `/code-of-conduct` |
| Change Requests | `/change-requests` |

Configured in `siteConfig.footerPolicyLinks`.
